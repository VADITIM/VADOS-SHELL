# Frontend rules

- **Tokens only.** Colours from `Appearance.color`, type from `Appearance.font` (Space Mono; labels in caps with `labelSpacing`), sizes from the per-feature `Appearance` groups. A raw hex or pixel size in a module is a bug.
- **The taskbar speaks sans** (user, 2026-11-13): every text on the taskbar, its idle pills, activity pill, tips and the extension pages uses `Appearance.font.sans`, the clock too since 2026-11-17 (user): time semibold at `Appearance.font.taskbarClock` and date in white at `clockDateOpacity`, both with tabular figures (`Appearance.font.tabularFigures`) so rolling digits never shift, in `Appearance.color.clockText`, which stays light in white pill mode since the clock floats over the wallpaper (2026-11-19), and a 1 px raised shade (`Appearance.color.clockShade`) that keeps them readable over any wallpaper.
- **Corners are squircle or fully round** (V/AS law 4). Pills use `radius: height / 2`.
- **The battery ring marks the cap** (user, 2026-11-17): while the charge limit is on (`Battery.cap`), a white arc, on the ring itself, a gap after the limit and before the top, where the charge starts (`Appearance.taskbar.chargeGap`, degrees), fills the locked part from the limit (80%) to full. The dashboard's battery bar marks it the same way (user, 2026-11-19): a light segment filling the bar from the top down to the limit, a gap (`Appearance.dashboard.battery.capGap`) only between it and the charge (user, 2026-11-19), under a larger percentage (`percent`).
- **An extended pill is two pills, top and bottom** (user, 2026-11-19): the top pill lists (devices, streams, networks, profiles), vertically centred and scrolling only when they overflow; the bottom pill holds the header (glyph, title, buttons such as OTHER, OFF, MUTE), Audio's volume bar and every interaction such as the Wi-Fi password prompt. Once the body has lifted, the bottom pill splits out of the top one through the liquid neck (`IdlePill.split`, `Motion.extension.split`) and merges back before the body lowers; each page says how tall its bottom content is (`footHeight`) and lists in `height - footHeight - Appearance.extension.splitRoom`. Every system pill (audio, Wi-Fi, power) behaves the same. Extended pills are 440 px wide. Nothing on the taskbar draws over an extended pill (user, 2026-11-19): the idle pills (their liquid body too, so nothing shows through the split's gap), the clock and the activity pill with its dots leave while the page covers them (`Taskbar.isUnderExtension`, `ActivityPill.isCovered`); an alert or the clipboard still shows.
- **The power page ends with PROTECT BATTERY** (user, 2026-11-16): a toggle at its bottom, under its own divider, for `battery.limit`; it shows only where the limit can be set (`Battery.canLimit`).
- **The power profiles are tinted** (user, 2026-11-17): PERFORMANCE red, BALANCED blue, POWER SAVER green (`Appearance.extension.profileColors`), on each row's glyph and the active dot; the label stays white.
- **REFRESH reads only numbers** (user, 2026-11-17): the Displays tab's refresh rates are an `OptionGroup` labelled `REFRESH (HZ)` whose choices are bare numbers (`60`, `50`); with more rates than fit it scrolls and drags inside itself like every option row.
- **The accent is picked in General > COLORS** (user, 2026-11-16; layout user, 2026-11-19): two columns; on the left the swatch and a narrow hex field (ACCENT), MARK under them and PILLS (DARK | WHITE, `pills.tone`) under MARK; on the right the wheel with FOLLOW THE ACCENT centred under it. Not on the User tab; the swatch, while pressed in, points the wheel at the accent instead of the selection colour.
- **White pills** (user, 2026-11-19): `pills.tone` `white` switches `Appearance.color` `panel`, `text`, `textBody`, `textMuted`, `textFaint`, `hairline` and `wash` to their light-body values (`isWhite`), eased over `Motion.stateDuration`; modules never branch on it, they only read those tokens. `pills.color` still wins for the body.
- **Settings > General holds the PILL group, the colours and nothing else** (user, 2026-11-17): PILL is a group heading (`Section.isGroup`, `groupTitle`) over its sub sections MOTION & ANIMATIONS and PLACEMENT; WINDOW MODE sits on Displays above MONITOR; NOTIFICATIONS, BATTERY and CAPTURE sit on Advanced with the other services. **Advanced has two sub tabs** (user, 2026-11-20): SERVICES (`pageKey` `services`, the services sections) and VAD/OS (`pageKey` `vados`, the LINK TO VAD/OS app cards in one column). The page heading types with the symbol typewriter (`ScrambleText`).
- **The User tab's GitHub box has three slides** (user, 2026-11-17): GITHUB HISTORY, FAVORITES (up to 5 cards side by side, the pinned repositories until picked with EDIT, `profile.favorites`) and RECENT PUSHES (up to 15 cards, 5 visible, a sideways `Flickable` with `Drift`); two small squircle arrows, one on each side, in a translucent grey with an accent arrow, step back and forth through them (user, 2026-11-19); the box and the GitHub name are centred under the picture and system details, and every card opens its page through `xdg-open`. CONNECT always sits at the right end of the GitHub name row, reading CONNECTED once `gh` is signed in (user, 2026-11-19): it opens a terminal running `gh auth login --web` (`GitHub.connect`, `Hub.connectCommand`) and reloads the profile when it closes. The picture enters with a spin and pop (`avatarSpin`, `Motion.settings.avatarPop`) and its ring is the accent.
- **The User tab is one centred column** (user, 2026-11-16), centred between the main bar and the body's far edge so both gaps match, measured on the widest row (the system values included, `ProfileEditor.blockWidth`) (`Settings.profileLead`, user, 2026-11-19): the name (and last name) on top, the picture under it, the GitHub name and history below. The name shows as an ASCII banner in the Def Leppard FIGlet font, centred in the column; the second name sits under it as small sans text (`secondSize`), never a banner (user, 2026-11-19); the system details are `infoSize` 16 (user, 2026-11-19); settings shows no version in its corner (user, 2026-11-19); Tab in the name field moves to the second name, both slicing in and out per character like the home banner (`SliceText`); the picture is round and spins in and out around its centre without scaling, fading from 0 to full on enter and back out on leave, no overshoot (`avatarSpin`, `Motion.settings.avatarPop`); the system rows type with the symbol typewriter (`ScrambleText`, every row finishing within `Motion.settings.infoType` whatever its length: `typeBudget` tightens the stagger) and are never cut: a long value runs on past the history's right edge; each field sits above its own banner, both fields on one line, hidden until the banner is hovered or edited (user, 2026-11-18; `Banner`, `utils/Figlet.js`). The banners never push the picture, details or history down: they stack upward from the picture at a fixed size (user, 2026-11-18): the name at `bannerSize`, the second name at half of it (`secondShare`), never scaled to the room. While editing, the banners stay where they are and only the field being edited shows (`editingField`), centred above the name banner, the two fields stacked there (name over second name, user, 2026-11-18), and its banner redraws live; the empty second field reads "Second Name". The picture sits at the history's left edge with the system beside it (user, 2026-11-17): OS, KERNEL, COMPOSITOR, SHELL, HOST, UPTIME, CPU, GPU, MEMORY (`infoSize`) (`utils/About.js`, `Machine`).
- **A resting alert never wraps** (user, 2026-11-17): title and message stay on one line until hovered and fade out at their far end (`Appearance.alerts.fade`, a soft `Masked`), never an ellipsis; the text leaves (`Motion.alerts.faceLeave`) before the pill starts closing (`shrinkDelay`). Width and height close together and land at the same time (`Motion.alerts.shrinkLag` 0, user, 2026-11-19).
- **An alert reads like its app** (user, 2026-11-19): its badge shows the app's own icon (`Notifications.appIconOf`), the catalogue glyph only when there is none; title and message are sans; an image is contained whole (`PreserveAspectFit`, at most `Appearance.alerts.imageHeight` tall) and the pill takes the image's width, never cropping it.
- **The screenshot toolbar does not animate in or out** (user, 2026-11-19): it is there at once and gone at once (`Motion.capture.grow`, `shrink`, `lag` 0; its tools have no enter).
- **A fader's buttons** (user, 2026-11-16): − and + at the two ends of its bar in faint white (`textMuted`), RESET in the middle of its label row in a faint red (`Action.tint`); the label and the value sit vertically centred in that row, level with RESET, so the bar is not pushed away from them (user, 2026-11-17).
- **Settings content is sans** (user, 2026-11-16): field and option labels (not the options inside a toggle, which stay mono), values, monitor and module names, link rows use `Appearance.font.sans`; section titles and action buttons stay mono. The lock module column has no group headings. APPLY / RESET on Displays and System Font are centred.
- **Settings say no more than they must** (user, 2026-11-15). No explanation lines under a choice or a canvas (what a toggle does, how to drag); a line of data stays (a monitor's description, why a layout cannot apply). Section titles enter with the label reveal (`LabelReveal`, the accent sweep). Fields and the grid editors are opaque (`Appearance.color.raised`), never a translucent wash over the ASCII background. A row that does not fit its section scrolls inside itself (`OptionGroup` turns into a drifting horizontal `Flickable`), it never runs past the section. The monitor canvas has no box of its own: its faint grid covers the whole settings pill, masked to its shape (user, 2026-11-17), and the monitors fit inside the tab's area; it shows the grid every `Appearance.settings.displays.grid` output pixels (120, hidden when its lines would crowd) and a dropped monitor slides along its neighbour onto that grid unless an edge or centre is within `snap` (user, 2026-11-17), monitors are static sharp rectangles (no press, glide or fling; user, 2026-11-17, an exception to the squircle corners) that follow the grid while dragged, and a clicked monitor moves one output pixel per arrow key (Shift: one grid step; `DisplayEditor.nudge`); it zooms with the wheel around the pointer and pans by dragging empty space; APPLY and RESET sit in the right column under the sections. The right column never moves while a monitor is picked (user, 2026-11-17): REFRESH (HZ) always stacks under its label, the description reserves two lines, and the column centres on the tallest height it has had. The home banner is centred on the screen's height in the accent colour, and slices in and out diagonally character by character, each character pulled on its own like a spring from a random spot anywhere in the settings menu and out to another, new spots on every open and close (`SliceText`, user, 2026-11-17); the pointer pushes the characters near it away, the closer the further, easing back as it leaves (`look.pushReach`, `pushDepth`, `pace.push`; user, 2026-11-17). Every 3 to 5 seconds (user, 2026-11-19) a symbol-typewriter wave runs through the banner column by column from left to right, a wide band (`sweepSpin`) that stretches furthest along the middle row and narrows towards the top and bottom (`sweepStretch`, `SliceText.sweepOf`), on the home banner and the User tab's name banner alike (`SliceText.hasSweep`, `Motion.settings.banner.sweep*`, `nameBanner.sweep*`), idle ambience that rests under reduced motion; the banner is drawn at `Appearance.settings.home.bannerSize` 32 (1.5x, user, 2026-11-18).
- **The taskbar sits on the left or right edge** (`taskbar.side`, picked in Settings > General under PLACEMENT; the Customization > Taskbar tab is gone, user, 2026-11-17) in one of three styles (`floating`, `fused`, `bare`, `taskbar.style`, set only in `shell.json` since then). There is no `...` pill. Taskbar code is written for the left edge only; anything readable inside it gets an `Upright` counter-flip bound to `host.isMirrored`. Its sizes were cut 10% on 2026-11-05 (thickness 47, items 40) and restored on 2026-11-10 (thickness 52, items 44), when the text looked blurry.
- **Text renders natively** (user, 2026-11-10): `shell.qml` sets `//@ pragma NativeTextRendering`, because Qt's default distance-field text is soft at the shell's small sizes on scale-1 screens. Fonts are body 15, label 13, chip 12 (raised from 13, 11, 10 on 2026-11-11, when the user found most strings small, pixelated and barely readable); feature sizes in `Appearance` grew with them.
- **The battery pill shows its charge at a glance** (user, 2026-11-15): a thin ring around its glyph (`Appearance.taskbar.chargeRing`) filled to the charge from the top, coloured by `Appearance.chargeColor` like the dashboard and the widget, over a faint track of the same colour.
- **The background apps pill leads the taskbar** (user, 2026-11-19): the tray items live in `TrayIdle` above the task pills, shown only while there are any, opening to the right on hover; the activity pill has no tray.
- **The idle pills follow `taskbar.order`** (user, 2026-11-19), dragged in Settings > General > ARRANGEMENT (`PillOrder.js`); a change slides each pill to its slot. Each row ends in a switch that removes its pill from the taskbar (`taskbar.hidden`, user, 2026-11-19); a removed row stays in the list, faded.
- **Dragging down, or scrolling down, on the idle pills compacts them** to the pills under the fold and a small dot (bottom to top); the fold is the divider in Settings > General > ARRANGEMENT, dragged like a pill (`PillOrder.fold`, user, 2026-11-19; before SETTINGS by default, so SETTINGS and POWER stay); dragging up, or pressing the dot, expands them again.
- **The activity pill sits in the middle of the taskbar with four states** (user, 2026-11-06): hidden (none), idle (visible, nothing happening), active (something running: Discord, Spotify), expanded (it spreads from the middle of the taskbar towards the screen into a bigger shape of its own; every module, Discord, Spotify and the rest, has its own expanded view). `CTRL + LEFT` / `CTRL + RIGHT` step through the states. **It has exactly three tabs** (user, 2026-11-19): NOTIFICATIONS, MUSIC and AUDIO/INPUT (the Discord call) (`Activities.tabs`, top to bottom; order user, 2026-11-19); background apps live in their own pill, recording stays a transient lead outside the tabs. Every tab other than the lead is a small liquid dot pill above or under the pill in tab order (`utils/ActivityTabs.js`); scrolling up merges the nearest dot from below into the pill and shoots the old lead out as a dot to its top (down mirrors it), and a click on a dot moves straight to that tab. Scrolling stops at the first and last tab, it never wraps around (user, 2026-11-19). A dot whose tab is live but not the lead is closed-active (user, 2026-11-19): it grows to `Appearance.activity.liveDot`, bigger than an idle dot and smaller than the pill, and shows that tab's animated face (the music bars, the call's mic, the unread count). Each dot keeps `dotGap` to its own neighbour, so a small dot beside a live one sits as close as any other (user, 2026-11-19; `ActivityPill.innerRestOf`). Expanding plays the symbol typewriter (`ScrambleText`) on every string, at the smaller `Appearance.activity.expanded.smallLabel` / `smallBody`; AUDIO/INPUT expanded has the same layout as MUSIC expanded. Mute and deafen (call) and back, play/pause, next (music) sit above the expanded pill as separate liquid pills, and a row of tab pills sits under it; both bud out of the pill and merge back into it with a small snap bounce (`ActivityBuds`, `Motion.activity.bud*`).
- **The accent is white by default and user-set** from settings. Selected and active state use `Appearance.color.accent`, never a fixed colour; a chosen settings option uses `Appearance.choice` (its own outline or pill colour, the accent when unset).

SCREEN OFF AFTER sits under the grid at its right edge, the file name and count at its left. While editing, the Lock Screen tab shows only the main frame: the neighbours and the + tiles fade out and take no taps; with edit mode off they show (user, 2026-11-20). The WALLPAPER field on top at the grid's left edge, as wide as the Wallpaper tab's FOLDER field (user, 2026-11-19), the 16 x 16 grid over the lock wallpaper at the screen's aspect and centred on the screen like the widget frame, and the modules as separate liquid pills shot out of the body's wall into a scrollable column beside it (user, 2026-11-16), never overlapping.
