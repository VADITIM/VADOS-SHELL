# Interaction rules

Enforced by `tools/check-rules.py` where marked ✓; the rest is checked in review.

- **Every full-screen surface is an overlay** ✓. It is an `OverlayWindow` (`windows/OverlayWindow.qml`) with a key in `Overlays.kinds` (`state/Overlays.qml`), which is the built overlay rows of `utils/Features.js` (`rules/features.md`). Overlays on the same layer exclude each other; opening one closes the others on its layer or below. Nothing else sets `WlrLayershell.keyboardFocus` (except the taskbar while a field asks for text, below) or the overlay layer.
- **The frame debugger is the one surface outside `Overlays`** (`windows/FrameWindow.qml`, `state/FrameDebug.qml`): it must not close what it inspects. It owns the keyboard while held, on the screen that started it, and `Escape` always resumes.
- **A taskbar field borrows the keyboard** (2026-11-14). While an extension page shows a text field (the Wi-Fi password), `TaskbarWindow` takes the keyboard exclusively through `Extension.isTyping` and nothing else; it lets go the moment the field closes, and an overlay opening takes the taskbar away, which closes the field first.
- **The password prompt borrows the keyboard** (user, 2026-11-18). It opens out of the activity pill exactly like the clipboard (the same body and motion, `ActivityState.openPrompt`), and while it is open `TaskbarWindow` takes the keyboard exclusively; over an open menu it shows as a plain panel in `windows/PromptPanelWindow.qml` on the overlay layer, which then holds the keyboard and never closes the menu. A click outside it, Escape or CANCEL cancels the request (`Passwords.cancel`, which logs why); the request ending closes it. **It stays above everything in the shell** (user, 2026-11-18): while it is open the taskbar sits on the overlay layer, no menu takes the keyboard on its screen (`OverlayWindow.hasKeyboard`), and a menu that opens under it maps first, the panel unmapping for `Motion.prompt.restack` and mapping again on top (`PromptPanelWindow.restack`).
- **A note borrows the keyboard** (2026-11-16). `WidgetWindow` sets `keyboardFocus` OnDemand only while a widget that takes text (`Widgets.js` `takesText`, the notes) is hovered or typed in (`WidgetLayer.wantsKeyboard`), None otherwise; never Exclusive.
- **One keyboard owner.** The topmost overlay (`Overlays.top`) owns the keyboard, and only while the pointer is on its screen (user, 2026-11-11: a menu only eats the click on its own monitor; other monitors keep their windows and driftwm keybinds). `OverlayWindow.hasKeyboard` drops the exclusive grab when `Compositor.focusedScreen` leaves the overlay's screen and takes it back on return; without `focusTracking` it always holds; Escape closes the topmost first; closing hands focus back to the window that had it (the compositor does this once the exclusive layer lets go). Every overlay is fully usable from the keyboard: arrows, Tab, Enter, Escape.
- **The taskbar is gathered and returned only by `Overlays`.** Switching overlays keeps the parts of the first gather; the taskbar comes back only when no overlay is left on its screen.
- **Every drag, pull, swipe or fling is a `Gesture`** ✓ (`components/Gesture.qml`) or a type built on it (`FoldDrag`). The finger drives; release only decides (`thrown` carries the velocity). Taps are `onTapped`, never `onClicked`, which also fires after a drag ✓. No hand-written press tracking in `onPositionChanged` ✓.
- **Every surface with an enter and a leave runs its phases through a `Lifecycle`** ✓ (`components/Lifecycle.qml`): no loose `isLeaving` / `isOpened` booleans set by hand, no `isShown ? start() : finish()`. Phase changes come only from `send(event)`; animations report their end from a final `ScriptAction`, never from `onStopped`. A phase that outlives `Motion.lifecycle.stuck` settles to a valid end and logs it. Every leave lands exactly once (`land()`), settled or not. Each overlay reports its phase to `Overlays`; a screen whose surfaces are all idle always gets its taskbar back, and a screen that goes away takes its overlays with it (`keepScreens`).
- **Every scrolling view drifts** (user, 2026-11-11). A `Flickable`, `ListView` or `GridView` the user scrolls declares a `Drift` inside it, and a keyboard move that scrolls it goes through `glideTo` or `glideAround`, never a direct `contentY` or `positionViewAtIndex` jump, unless the next line reads where the row landed (the task manager places its menu at the selected row). The drift stays subtle: no visible overshoot, a short glide.
- **Everything is interruptible.** Any ability can be re-triggered mid-flight and continues from where it is: `Mutate` keeps its pop, `Burst` rewinds a leave. Nothing visibly snaps back to zero.
- **Only `Attention` decides what may interrupt** ✓ (`state/Attention.qml`). Critical notifications always peek; do-not-disturb holds the rest; a fullscreen window holds normal ones; low ones never peek; arrivals within `Motion.attention.batch` share one peek; a peek lasts the notification's own timeout or `Motion.attention.peek`, and a critical one until dismissed. A hovered alert opens to its whole content (image included) and never goes while hovered; once the pointer leaves it retracts and goes `Motion.attention.linger` (1.2 s) later (user, 2026-11-11). Held notifications wait in the activity bar, never lost.
- **The clipboard opens anywhere** (user, 2026-11-16): over an open menu it shows as a plain panel in `windows/ClipboardDismissWindow.qml` on the overlay layer and never closes the menu. A click anywhere outside it closes it at once (that window is a full-screen click catcher while it is open).
- **A click outside the expanded activity pill folds it** (user, 2026-11-19): while it is expanded on a screen, the same click catcher as the clipboard (`windows/ClipboardDismissWindow.qml`) covers that screen except the pill and its control and tab buds, and a press there folds it (`ActivityState.fold`); not while a menu is open on that screen. An extended system pill that covers it folds it too, so it stays folded once the system pill closes (`ActivityPill.foldRequested`).
- **The search key closes the clipboard first** (user, 2026-11-13). While the clipboard history is open on the focused screen, the search shortcut and `search toggle` only close it; the launcher opens on the next press (`Shortcuts.toggleSearch`).
- **Every slider is the V/AS drag-only slider** (user, 2026-11-16): `components/Slider.qml`, for settings faders, volume and brightness alike. A tap never changes the value; horizontal travel past the slop does, relative to the value at the crossing, a whole track's reach being the whole range. Every slider looks like the taskbar's volume bar: a round bar with a solid accent fill and no knob, and from the press on (not only once dragged) the whole bar doubles its height, growing up and down equally; the fill eases only while no finger drives it. Each slider has − and + at its two ends where there is room, and RESET under it at the right where the setting has a default.
- **Super and a click on empty space close a menu** (user, 2026-11-17). The search key while any overlay is open on the focused screen closes the topmost one and never opens the launcher (`Shortcuts.toggleSearch`); a click on any gap between the pills of a surface closes it, so a surface blocks clicks only over its own pills and content, never over its whole body (settings: the nav bar, the sub bar and the content area). Escape closes every overlay.
- **Settings moves through its tabs with the arrows** (user, 2026-11-18): Up and Down step through the focused bar (`Settings.isSubFocused`), Left and Right move between the main bar and the sub bar (mirrored with the taskbar), Tab and Shift+Tab step the main tabs; a click on a tab gives the keyboard back to settings.
- **Ctrl+Z in the screenshot takes back every change** (user, 2026-11-19): a stroke drawn, a shape or arrow carried or reshaped, an erase, a text placed or edited, each one step (`Ink.recorded`, `Ink.undone`); a press that changed nothing records nothing. The area selection is not part of the history.
- **Ctrl+C in a screenshot text copies the shot unless text is selected** (user, 2026-11-19): with nothing selected in the text field it finishes the text and copies the screenshot (`cut`); with a selection it copies that text.
- **The screenshot toolbar drags along the taskbar edge** (user, 2026-11-19): holding it and dragging moves it up and down, kept on the screen (`Screenshot.toolbarShift`, reset on each open); a tap still picks the tool under it, a drag never does.
