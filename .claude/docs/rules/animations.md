# Animation rules

V/AS `animation/` is binding; these are this shell's additions.

- **Every duration, curve parameter and physics constant is a named token in `config/Motion.qml`** (sizes in `Appearance.qml`). Nothing inline except one-off jolt keyframes.
- **Animations are ability components.** An animation is a component you attach to an element; it plays from that element's state (enter, leave, idle, haptic, click, right click, drag) and the host only sets state. New motion is written as an ability; abilities live in `components/` (`.claude/docs/architecture.md` § Abilities). A host never calls `restart()` on its own timeline; a `Behavior` easing a property towards a state value is allowed.
- **Every element has an enter and a leave; the leave is its own, faster animation, never the enter reversed.** Enter/leave of pieces goes through `components/Turn.qml`.
- **Pills and the pop bar mutate** (`.claude/docs/project-notes.md` § Mutation): reshape in place, own enter/leave, animate inner element changes. Mutation timing comes from `Motion.pill`, kept fast (pop 60 ms, spread 110 ms, leave pop 130 ms as of 2026-11-05). Items inside a spreading pill pop in from the centre outwards, never all at once; the outermost plays only after the pill has reached its full length (2026-11-06).
- **One liquid engine** (user, 2026-11-06). No feature gets its own liquid physics. Every spring, bounce and wobble of a liquid body is a character in `Motion.liquid` (`response`, `damping`, `expansion`) played through `utils/Liquid.js`; necks merge and separate through `Motion.liquid.neck`. A new surface (alerts, clipboard, lock, notifications) picks a character or adds one, so the whole desktop shares one physical identity.
- **Shared geometry lives in `utils/Geometry.js`** ✓. Components do not write their own clamps, smoothsteps, mixes or splines; a new geometric primitive is added there with a test.
- **Reduced motion** (`motion.reduced`, V/AS `animation/08-degradation.md`). Every `duration` in `components/` and `modules/` goes through `Motion.pace`, the one precedence function (enforced by `tools/check-rules.py`). Under it transitions are instant, idle ambience (`Rising`, splashes, crests) does not run, content stays (`TypedText` keeps typing, drags keep following the finger), and exits are as instant as enters. Interaction timers are not motion and are not paced (`Motion.reveal.dwell`).
- **Liquid is one material.** Anything that should read as liquid lives in a `LiquidLayer`'s `shapes` (blur + threshold goo) so it necks and fuses with its neighbours. Droplets, splashes and bands are `LiquidShape`s or `Shape`s inside that layer, never separate coloured overlays.
- **Every shell interaction happens and mutates from the taskbar**, so the shell reads as one unified system. The dashboard is the foundation every overlay adapts: the same liquid `Burst` enter and leave out of the taskbar column (search does; settings is left as it is for now, 2026-11-05).
- **Overlays absorb the taskbar.** Everything on the taskbar reacts and merges with an overlay that opens over it: its parts merge into one pill that becomes the overlay (settings: into the gear, then to the centre; dashboard and search: the parts stream to the taskbar column and form a new circle there, fused to the screen edge, which did not exist before, then it explodes along the dashboard away from the edge, its whole root side curving into the edge like the search lobe, bouncing out past the far wall and back, with waves crashing onto its walls). The taskbar comes back when the overlay has left; the dashboard and search instead hand the taskbar back as their leave starts, shrinks while sliding out past the edge in one motion, without stopping at the edge, and the pills it passes swell and goo with it.
- **Overlays have rising bubbles, not idle liquid motion.** While open, bubbles rise off the body to the screen edges (`Rising`); the body itself stays still.
- **Search is liquid, not fire**, and opens with the dashboard's burst (the earlier two-lobe clash is gone, 2026-11-05).
- **Press feedback** is the `Press` ability: scale down on press over `Motion.press.down` with OutQuad, back over `Motion.stateDuration` with OutBack; depths from `Motion.press`.
