# Performance rules

Checked in review and with the preview; ✓ is enforced by a test. The shell must cost nothing while idle.

- **Idle costs nothing.** No `FrameAnimation`, `Timer`, process or poll runs while its surface is hidden: services poll only while a window sets `isActive` (`Hardware`, `Traffic`, `Weather`), abilities stop their frame loops when inactive (`Rising.isActive`, `Burst.isActive`), overlay windows are invisible while not present.
- **Frame budget** ✓: script work per frame stays under `Motion.budget.frame` (4 ms) for `Motion.budget.sampleShare` (95%) of frames during the heaviest animation (the search burst, opening and leaving), measured by `tools/tests/tst_budget.qml` (best of three sweeps, bindings and `trace()` together). It measures script only, offscreen; render cost on real hardware is not measured yet. Geometry that depends on several changing values is traced once per frame through `Qt.callLater`, never as chains of bindings over arrays (the burst's wall outline moved into `trace()` for this, 2026-11-07).
- **Only the rendering components own effects** ✓. `MultiEffect`, `ShaderEffect`, `ShaderEffectSource`, `layer.enabled`, `layer.effect` and masks live in `components/LiquidLayer.qml` (goo) and `components/Masked.qml` (masks); everything else composes them. A new effect becomes a rendering component, joins `RENDERING_COMPONENTS` in `tools/check-rules.py` and gets its passes into `.claude/docs/architecture.md` § Rendering system. `clip` and `Shape` stay where they are used.
- **Liquid layers exist only while shown** ✓. Every `LiquidLayer` says when with `isShown` (required; never `visible`), and renders only while shown and not transparent: off means invisible, textures not `live` and layers disabled, never just hidden (a rendering component never writes `layer.enabled: true` or `live: true`, `tst_rendering`). Keep it unshown when nothing is in it, and never stack two full-screen layers that are both shown.
- **One rendering precedence** ✓ (`tst_rendering`): `rendering.quality` (`full`, `lite`) through `config/Rendering.qml`. Blur radii go through `Rendering.blur`, as durations go through `Motion.pace`; under lite the liquid keeps its shapes and threshold and drops every blur pass (V/AS `animation/08-degradation.md`). Quality and reduced motion are independent: motion decides timing, quality decides passes; reduced motion also stops idle bubbles and splashes, and both together are the cheapest path.
