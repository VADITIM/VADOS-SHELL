# Frontend rules

- **Tokens only.** Colours from `Appearance.color`, type from `Appearance.font` (Space Mono; labels in caps with `labelSpacing`), sizes from the per-feature `Appearance` groups. A raw hex or pixel size in a module is a bug.
- **The taskbar speaks sans** (user, 2026-11-13): every text on the taskbar, its idle pills, activity pill, tips and the extension pages uses `Appearance.font.sans`; only the clock (time and date) keeps Space Mono.
- **Corners are squircle or fully round** (V/AS law 4). Pills use `radius: height / 2`.
- **The battery ring marks the cap** (user, 2026-11-17): while the charge limit is on (`Battery.cap`), a white arc, on the ring itself, a gap after the limit and before the top, where the charge starts (`Appearance.taskbar.chargeGap`, degrees), fills the locked part from the limit (80%) to full.
- **The power page ends with PROTECT BATTERY** (user, 2026-11-16): a toggle at its bottom, under its own divider, for `battery.limit`; it shows only where the limit can be set (`Battery.canLimit`).
- **The power profiles are tinted** (user, 2026-11-17): PERFORMANCE red, BALANCED blue, POWER SAVER green (`Appearance.extension.profileColors`), on each row's glyph and the active dot; the label stays white.
- **REFRESH reads only numbers** (user, 2026-11-17): the Displays tab's refresh rates are an `OptionGroup` labelled `REFRESH (HZ)` whose choices are bare numbers (`60`, `50`); with more rates than fit it scrolls and drags inside itself like every option row.
- **The accent is picked in General > COLORS** (user, 2026-11-16): the swatch and hex field sit at the top of that section, not on the User tab; the swatch, while pressed in, points the section's wheel at the accent instead of the selection colour.
- **The User tab is one centred column** (user, 2026-11-16): the name (and last name) on top, the picture under it, the GitHub name and history below.
- **A fader's buttons** (user, 2026-11-16): − and + at the two ends of its bar in faint white (`textMuted`), RESET in the middle of its label row in a faint red (`Action.tint`).
- **Settings content is sans** (user, 2026-11-16): field and option labels (not the options inside a toggle, which stay mono), values, monitor and module names, link rows use `Appearance.font.sans`; section titles and action buttons stay mono. The lock module column has no group headings. APPLY / RESET on Displays and System Font are centred.
- **Settings say no more than they must** (user, 2026-11-15). No explanation lines under a choice or a canvas (what a toggle does, how to drag); a line of data stays (a monitor's description, why a layout cannot apply). Section titles enter with the label reveal (`LabelReveal`, the accent sweep). Fields, the monitor canvas and the grid editors are opaque (`Appearance.color.raised`), never a translucent wash over the ASCII background. A row that does not fit its section scrolls inside itself (`OptionGroup` turns into a drifting horizontal `Flickable`), it never runs past the section. The monitor canvas fills the tab's height, zooms with the wheel around the pointer and pans by dragging empty space; APPLY and RESET sit in the right column under the sections. The home banner is centred on the screen's height in the accent colour.
- **The taskbar sits on the left or right edge** (`taskbar.side`, from settings) in one of three styles (`floating`, `fused`, `bare`), chosen in settings and saved to `shell.json`. There is no `...` pill. Taskbar code is written for the left edge only; anything readable inside it gets an `Upright` counter-flip bound to `host.isMirrored`. Its sizes were cut 10% on 2026-11-05 (thickness 47, items 40) and restored on 2026-11-10 (thickness 52, items 44), when the text looked blurry.
- **Text renders natively** (user, 2026-11-10): `shell.qml` sets `//@ pragma NativeTextRendering`, because Qt's default distance-field text is soft at the shell's small sizes on scale-1 screens. Fonts are body 15, label 13, chip 12 (raised from 13, 11, 10 on 2026-11-11, when the user found most strings small, pixelated and barely readable); feature sizes in `Appearance` grew with them.
- **The battery pill shows its charge at a glance** (user, 2026-11-15): a thin ring around its glyph (`Appearance.taskbar.chargeRing`) filled to the charge from the top, coloured by `Appearance.chargeColor` like the dashboard and the widget, over a faint track of the same colour.
- **Dragging down on the idle pills compacts them** to power, audio and a small dot (bottom to top); dragging up, or pressing the dot, expands them again.
- **The activity pill sits in the middle of the taskbar with four states** (user, 2026-11-06): hidden (none), idle (visible, nothing happening), active (something running: Discord, Spotify), expanded (it spreads from the middle of the taskbar towards the screen into a bigger shape of its own; every module, Discord, Spotify and the rest, has its own expanded view). `CTRL + LEFT` / `CTRL + RIGHT` step through the states.
- **The accent is white by default and user-set** from settings. Selected and active state use `Appearance.color.accent`, never a fixed colour; a chosen settings option uses `Appearance.choice` (its own outline or pill colour, the accent when unset).
- **Settings pages follow the user's sketches** (2026-11-13). Flat sections, never cards: a title, its rows, a hairline; sections in columns. Choices read `FULL | REDUCED`, the chosen one ringed (outline) or filled (pill), its colour from the wheel in its own column beside the Settings sections. The taskbar's clock stays in the body's bottom-left corner. Wallpaper is the first Customization sub tab, the current image large with its direct neighbours stacked above and below, and every image further away slid out past the top or bottom screen edge (user, 2026-11-15); Lock Screen is the second: SCREEN OFF AFTER on top, the 16 x 16 grid over the lock wallpaper at the screen's aspect and centred on the screen like the widget frame (SCREEN OFF AFTER and the WALLPAPER field above and below it at 60% of its width, centred; user, 2026-11-16), the WALLPAPER field at the bottom, and the modules as separate liquid pills shot out of the body's wall into a scrollable column beside it (user, 2026-11-16), never overlapping. The column clips one panel padding inside the pill at top and bottom, so no card pokes past the curve. Widgets (Customization) and Displays (Settings) use the same layout (user, 2026-11-16): their canvas fills the body (as high above as below, so the widget frame and the displays canvas are centred on the screen, and the option column is centred in its pill with hidden rows taking no height) and their option sections sit in that edge column. On Displays the monitor description and the field labels (RESOLUTION, REFRESH, SCALE, ROTATION) use the sans font and APPLY / RESET are large and centred in the column (user, 2026-11-16). The VAD/OS ASCII background runs behind the body and the column as one full-screen pattern, seen only through them.
- **The search is one liquid body growing out of the taskbar edge** (user's sketches, 2026-11-05 and 2026-11-06): a base column 100% of the screen height and 20% of its width; the list from the top (no favourites strip since 2026-11-12; pinned apps lead it while the field is empty), its rows fading and shrinking as they scroll up past the top, names in the sans font; the best match as a large ringed circle at mid-height, beside the list, with its name in a pill outside the body; the field at the bottom.
- **The far wall keeps the drawn curve**: one smooth S-wave traced from the sketch (`Appearance.search.wall`), not steps, shelves or bulge lobes. Change the points only from a new drawing.
- **The best-match circle always shows the best match** (user, 2026-11-11; the most recent app while the field is empty). **The profile floats beside the search as its own liquid blob** (user, 2026-11-11): it rises out of the circle at the taskbar's middle before the body bursts, on an arc through the bottom-left (user, 2026-11-12), and lands with a wave burst (swell and splash ring), at 20% of the screen height, large (232 px), and then holds still (no wobble since 2026-11-12); the profile picture (or the name's initial) is inside, the name below opens settings on USER, and the larger power options run top to bottom close on its left. **The space right of the profile is reserved for the V/AI agent widget** (user, 2026-11-12): nothing else goes there.
- **The best match is tinted by its app** (user, 2026-11-12): its squircle runs a 45° gradient from the icon's main colour to a slightly darker (light colours) or lighter (dark colours) shade, and the icon fills most of it.
- **Right click on an app opens a small, fast menu** (favourite, hide), never an instant action, and the choice never rebuilds or replays the list: only the row it touches changes. **Hidden apps** stay out of the search until the grey HIDDEN toggle at the very bottom is on (user, 2026-11-12).
- **List rows beside the best match shorten** (name elided at the end) so text never runs under it; no fade there.
- **App entries have no container**: the icon itself is the tile (1:1) and the name sits below it. No hint labels (`ENTER`, `RECENT`).
- **Overlays dim the screen**; blur is the compositor's job (Hyprland `layerrule`, driftwm window/layer rules).
- **Text arriving on screen uses the V/AS reveal** (`TypedText`, `Turn`), not a plain fade.
- **Hovered search targets scale a little** (user, 2026-11-16): list rows, the best-match circle, the best match's name pill (with its liquid shape; the tether starts past the raised circle), the profile picture and the power buttons raise by `Motion.press.raise` through `Press.isRaised` on hover or keyboard selection, never a separate hover scale.
- **Hover is not selection that sticks** (user, 2026-11-16): a search target the pointer leaves lets go of its selection (`Search.release`, zone `""`); the next arrow or Tab key starts again from the best match. The whole row, pill included, raises on hover.
